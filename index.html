About MyTertiary ZA

Independent platform for South African students.

MyTertiary ZA is designed to make tertiary education information easier to find for South African students. Instead of searching many different websites, students can use one platform to discover universities, explore qualifications, understand requirements, compare options, and quickly access official application portals.

MyTertiary ZA is an independent guidance platform and is not affiliated with the institutions listed. Always verify admission requirements, application deadlines, fees and other information directly with the institution before applying.
